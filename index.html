01About me

Curious builder, learning by shipping.

Based in Cape Town, South Africa, I'm a software engineering student currently working through HyperionDev's software engineering bootcamp. My path into code started with curiosity about how the websites and apps I use every day actually work, and it quickly turned into wanting to be the person who builds them.

Alongside my studies I already build for real clients. I designed and developed the official website for a Cape Town performer — including a working online merch shop with card payments, automated invoice emails and a custom domain — and I'm building a full booking system with a Flask back end for a boutique guesthouse. I enjoy the whole journey: the design, the debugging, and the moment it goes live.

My goal is to grow into a well-rounded full-stack engineer who writes clean, tested code and understands the people it's for. I'm co-founding a small web and mobile development studio with two partners, and I'm looking for opportunities where I can keep learning from experienced engineers while contributing from day one.
